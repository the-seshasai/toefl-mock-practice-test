# Decisions

Ambiguities resolved in favor of the most faithful-to-real-TOEFL option.

- **D-01 Spec deviations from the baseline prompt.** Speaking = 7 Listen-and-Repeat + 4 Interview questions (11 items). Writing = 10 Build a Sentence + 1 Email + 1 Academic Discussion. Listening Announcement and Conversation counts follow the ETS blueprint ranges. Source: ETS 2026 blueprint PDF.
- **D-02 Scoring.** Overall = average of four sections rounded to nearest 0.5 (ETS wording), not just "average".
- **D-03 Reading stage split.** ETS does not publish router/stage item counts. We use router ≈ half the items, second stage lower/upper, capped at 50 items / ~30 min.
- **D-04 Per-task timers inside a section.** ETS publishes section times only; per-task splits (e.g. Build a Sentence ≈ 35 s each) are our estimates.
- **D-05 Passage lengths.** Blueprint says spec texts reach ~200 words; real practice passages are longer. Advanced Reading passages may exceed 200 words.
