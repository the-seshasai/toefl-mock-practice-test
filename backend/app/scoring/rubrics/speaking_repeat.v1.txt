Listen and Repeat - scoring rubric v1 (applied by code, not by an LLM).

The recording is transcribed locally and aligned word by word to the target sentence.
accuracy = correctly repeated words (in order) / max(words in target, words heard)

Score 0-5 by the highest threshold reached (minimum accuracy, score):
0.95 5
0.85 4
0.70 3
0.50 2
0.25 1
0.00 0

5 - essentially perfect repetition; 4 - one small slip; 3 - meaning preserved but several words missing or changed;
2 - about half the sentence; 1 - a fragment; 0 - no usable response.
Delivery measures (speech rate, pauses, recognizer confidence) are reported for feedback but do not change the score.
